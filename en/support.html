Common questions

Why hasn’t free space increased after cleanup?

Ordinary files are moved to macOS Trash and still occupy disk space. Reclaimora does not empty Trash or permanently delete files. Before emptying Trash yourself, make sure you no longer need the files; the app cannot restore them afterwards.

How do I restore files moved to Trash?

Use Undo after cleanup, or restore from Cleanup History. The recorded file must still exist, match its recorded identity, have an available original location, and be accessible to the app. If in-app recovery fails, check the files in Finder’s Trash. Restore Photos library images in Photos → Recently Deleted.

Does deleting history delete files from Trash?

No. Deleting a cleanup record also removes its in-app restore entry, while the files remain in Trash. Keep the record if you want to keep that restore entry.

Why is a folder missing or a scan incomplete?

Check selected folders in Settings → Permissions & Safety and grant access again from the relevant feature. macOS privacy restrictions, file permissions, disconnected drives, or revoked access can prevent reading. Full Disk Access does not bypass App Sandbox. An unreadable folder is not necessarily empty.

Why doesn’t the App Store edition show all app-related files?

The App Store edition is limited by App Sandbox and user-granted access. It does not clean third-party preferences, containers, shared app data, startup items, or browser data. Results depend on accessible locations; the app does not promise to find every associated file.

How are similar photos deleted and restored?

Images in folders are moved to Trash as ordinary files. Photos library images are removed through the Photos framework and managed in Photos → Recently Deleted. Photos controls retention. Try the flow with disposable test photos first.

Why am I not receiving reminders?

Enable reminders in the app, then check Reclaimora in System Settings → Notifications and review your Focus settings. Notifications are optional; turning them off does not affect manual scans.

Why did history change after switching editions?

The App Store and directly distributed editions may use different local data directories. There is no automatic history migration. Keep the original edition and Trash files, avoid running both editions together, and complete any restoration before switching editions or changing access.

How do I update the app?

Update the Mac App Store edition through the App Store. The directly distributed edition has no in-app auto-updater; use only a trusted official release source with the same app identity. Interface screenshots on this website do not mean a new version is available in the store.
