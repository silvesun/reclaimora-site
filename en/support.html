Support

Found a problem or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub with your macOS version and what you were doing.

I removed something by mistake

Everything goes to the Trash. Restore it from Cleanup History in Reclaimora, or drag it out of the Trash. Removed photos are in the Photos app's Recently Deleted album.

A folder isn't scanned

Reclaimora only reads folders you choose. Choose the folder again in Reclaimora, or grant Full Disk Access in System Settings › Privacy & Security for Mail attachments.

Reminders don't appear

Allow notifications for Reclaimora in System Settings › Notifications.
