Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026 · Applies to Reclaimora 1.0 for macOS

Reclaimora is a disk cleaner that runs entirely on your Mac. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

No data collection

Reclaimora has no network access. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or other third-party code, and it never sends any information off your Mac. We do not track you across apps or websites.

What stays on your Mac

To work, Reclaimora keeps a few things locally in its own sandboxed container: your settings, cleanup history and undo records, the list of items you chose to ignore, a scan cache that speeds up later scans, and access bookmarks for folders you chose. Deleting the app removes them.

Permissions, only when you use a feature

  • Folders (Home, Desktop, Documents, Downloads): only the folders you pick in the Open panel are scanned.
  • Photos library: read only when you ask Reclaimora to find similar photos. Photos you remove go to the Photos app's Recently Deleted album.
  • Notifications: only for reminders you turn on.
  • Full Disk Access: optional, granted by you in System Settings, used to list Mail attachment copies.

Deleting files

Everything Reclaimora removes goes to the Trash and can be restored from Cleanup History. Reclaimora never empties the whole Trash. Only if you confirm “Free Space Now” after a cache-only cleanup does it permanently delete the items that cleanup put there, and nothing else.

Children

Reclaimora collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
