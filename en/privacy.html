Privacy policy

Your files stay on your Mac. Here’s how local app processing differs from this website and support services.

Effective November 4, 2026

Scope

This policy applies to the Mac App Store sandboxed edition and the directly distributed edition of Reclaimora, and this support website. Updated November 4, 2026.

Data processing in the app

Reclaimora analyzes authorized files, storage usage, app-related files, and photos on your Mac. The app contains no advertising SDK, telemetry, file uploads, or in-app network requests. Scanned files, file names, results, and cleanup records are not transmitted to the developer.

What stays on your Mac

Settings, scan caches, cleanup and restoration records, ignored items, and folder authorization bookmarks are stored locally. The App Store edition uses App Sandbox; the directly distributed edition may use a different data directory. Deleting the application itself does not guarantee removal of this local data. Use the app’s settings to manage records and access.

Permissions and store boundaries

File access depends on your selected folders and macOS permissions. Photos and optional notification permissions are requested when their features are used. The App Store edition does not clean third-party preferences, containers, shared app data, startup items, or browser data. Full Disk Access does not bypass App Sandbox. Browser-privacy, startup-item, and extension management pages are absent from the current navigation.

Cleanup and restoration

Ordinary cleanup moves files to system Trash, where they still occupy disk space. The app does not permanently delete files or empty Trash. Restoration requires the recorded file to remain available, match its recorded identity, have an available original location, and be accessible to the app. Deleting history removes the associated in-app restore entry; it does not delete Trash files. Photos manages library deletion and recovery in Recently Deleted.

Your controls

Revoke Photos and notification permissions in System Settings and select folders again from the app’s permission interface. You can manage ignored items and cleanup history. Revoking access may prevent scanning or restoration. To preserve recovery options, keep the original app, its restore records, and the files in Trash.

Support website and GitHub

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no analytics scripts, ads, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or custom submission forms. GitHub may process IP addresses and request logs when you visit; this is separate from the Reclaimora app’s on-device processing. GitHub Issues are submitted voluntarily, public by default, and processed by GitHub under its own privacy policy.

Contact and changes

The website and app support maintainer is silvesun. Submit support or privacy questions through GitHub Issues without including private files, account details, full paths, or unredacted records. Future changes to this policy will be published here with an updated date.
